1. Start the program.
2. Initialize all expense counts and totals to zero.
3. Enter an expense amount.
4. Check whether the amount is zero. If yes, go to Step 12.
5. If the amount is negative, print “Invalid Expense” and go to Step 11.
6. If the amount is less than ₹100, classify it as a Small expense and update its count and total.
7. If the amount is from ₹100 to less than ₹500, classify it as a Medium expense and update its count and total.
8. If the amount is ₹500 or more, classify it as a Large expense and update its count and total.
9. Update the total number of valid expenses and the total amount of valid expenses.
10. Repeat the process by entering another expense amount.
11. Go back to Step 3 to read the next expense.
12. Display the total number of valid expenses and the total amount spent.
13. Display the count and total amount for Small, Medium, and Large expenses.
14. If valid expenses exist, calculate and display the average expense; otherwise, print “No valid expenses entered.”
15. Stop the program.
