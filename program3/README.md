Start
Enter the purchase amount, customer type, and delivery distance.
If the customer is a student, give a 15% discount if the purchase amount is ₹2000 or more; otherwise, give a 10% discount.
If the customer is a regular customer, give an 8% discount if the purchase amount is ₹3000 or more; otherwise, give no discount.
Calculate the discount amount and subtract it from the purchase amount.
Check the delivery distance. If it is 5 km or less, charge ₹40. If it is more than 5 km but not more than 15 km, charge ₹70. Otherwise, charge ₹100.
Add the delivery charge to the amount after the discount to get the final payable amount.
Display the discount amount, delivery charge, and final payable amount.
Stop the program.
